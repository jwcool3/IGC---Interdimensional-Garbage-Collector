# 🏗️ Multi-Layer Crafting Chains - Deep Progression System

## 🎯 **Crafting Philosophy: Raw → Basic → Advanced → Mega Projects**

### **Layer 1**: Raw Resources → Basic Components
### **Layer 2**: Basic Components → Advanced Parts  
### **Layer 3**: Advanced Parts → Complex Systems
### **Layer 4**: Complex Systems → Mega Structures

---

## 🔧 **CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS CHAIN**

### **Layer 1: Basic Building Blocks**
```
🔩 Hull Piece
├── Input: 100 Metal Scraps + 20 Plastic
├── Output: 1 Hull Piece
└── Use: Basic construction unit

🔗 Structural Beam
├── Input: 80 Metal Scraps + 15 Scrap Metal + 10 Refined Metal

```

├── Input: 3 Hull Pieces + 1 Structural Beam + 30 Electronics

## 🎮 **STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS**

### **Resource Investment Scaling**
```
📊 Example: Small Ship Compartment Total Cost
├── 400 Metal Scraps (for 4 Hull Pieces)
├── 80 Plastic
├── 60 Scrap Metal  
├── 40 Refined Metal
├── 200 Electronics
├── 120 Rare Metals
├── 20 Advanced Alloy
├── 100 Compressed Metal
└── PLUS: Multiple facility requirements and processing time
```

### **Player Decision Points**
- **Immediate needs** vs **long-term mega projects**
- **Specialize** in one chain vs **diversify** across multiple
- **Resource efficiency** vs **time efficiency**
- **Manual crafting** vs **automated production**

### **Progression Gates**
- **Small Compartment**: First major milestone (expand ship)
- **Engineering Bay**: Unlocks advanced recipes
- **Power Plant**: Enables multiple facility operations
- **Automated Factory**: Passive progression begins
- **Dimensional Gateway**: Endgame content access

This creates a satisfying "numbers go up" progression while maintaining strategic depth! 🚀
